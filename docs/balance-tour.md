# Balance: whole tours (Tour workstream)

Generated by `npm run test:tour` (tools/balance/tour-runs.ts), 2000 tours per difficulty. Events: src/content/events (115 events).

**Skat is drawn from the measured play of the computer opponents** (src/tour/sim.ts `stubSkatModel`, numbers in
src/tour/skatstats.ts from docs/balance-skat.md): each figure declares, wins its declared games and picks Grand,
Null and Hand as measured against two medium figures; the declarer's win chance moves by
0.004 per list point the defenders are weaker or stronger than a medium figure; opponents at depth d play
as if their win chance were 0.1 × (d − 1) higher and their list rating 8 × (d − 1) points higher
(Gemütlich d = 0.6, Sportlich 1, Scharf 1.8; fitted to real AI rounds, tools/balance/tour-skat-check.ts,
results in docs/notes-tour.md "Stub against the real AI").
The stub reproduces every figure's measured list points within about 1 point. The player is `good`: declares
30 %, wins 77.5 %, 18.2 list points per game against two medium figures
(a good club player between the strong and the very strong figures). Policy: greedy route choice with one step of
look-ahead, buys fuel at the garage when low, souvenirs/upgrades by a fixed priority, answers events by expected
value. Minutes from contract §4.2 (inn 7.5 per 3 games, Preisskat 15 per 6 games, sudoku 3 / 10, drive 0.5).

Reading and decisions: docs/notes-tour.md (section Balance).
